THE FOUR CATEGORIES — STATUS
────────────────────────────
  1. Conditional attack/block gates ......... DONE
     - Archangel of Tithes attack tax broadened ("or planeswalkers you
       control"); NEW blockTax() helper enforced in resolveCombat
       (defender pays {1}/blocker or those blocks are removed).
     - Kefnet hand-size gate moved into canAttack/canBlock context so it
       applies to attacks AND blocks, player AND AI.
     - Scourge of the Throne: untap-all-attackers when attacking the
       most-life player, once per turn (scourgeUntapTurn).
     - Bident of Thassa activation + Kardur ETB: force_attack state flag
       drives pickAttackers(mustAttack); player-side compulsion toasts.
     - Twins of Discord: parity_ban state (odd/even MV can't block),
       deterministic parity choice, enforced in both blocker filters.

  2. Two-sentence "Choose target X. <effect>" ............ DONE
     - Parser gained a COMPOUND_PATTERNS merge pass: consecutive sentences
       matching a known compound shape are joined before classification
       (period-tolerant regexes — joinClean strips sentence periods).
     - 23 compound shapes wired to dedicated resolvers: Confiscation Coup,
       Jolted Awake, V.A.T.S., War in Heaven, Chaos Warp, Cryptek, Star
       Athlete, Tyrite Sanctum, Suspended Sentence, Slithermuse, Disorienting
       Choice, Benthic Anomaly, Yes Man, Forge of Heroes, Destiny Spinner,
       Cacophony Unleashed, Three Dog, Filigree Racer, Desdemona, Angelic
       Aberration, Silver Shroud Costume, The Black Gate, Veronica/Sauron
       loot shapes.

  3. Player-targeting ("choose an opponent", "target player") ... DONE
     - Solo-deterministic resolution: "target player/opponent" already
       parses as targets=0; new resolvers pick the opposing side.
     - Vial Smasher / The Lord of Pain (first_spell_damage): damage equal
       to the cast spell's MV on first-spell-each-turn.
     - Endurance (graveyard_to_library_bottom), Slithermuse (hand-size
       difference draw), Vraska −9 (poison_diff + poison SBA at 10),
       The Black Gate (unblockable-this-turn approximation), Yes Man
       (control handoff + quest counters), Disorienting Choice (ramp).

  4. Full sub-mechanics ................................... DONE
     - RING: full ladder implemented per LTR rules — temptations count +
       Ring-bearer per side; L1 bearer can't be blocked by greater power;
       L2 attack-loot (draw+discard, both sides); L3 blocked→blocker is
       sacrificed at end of combat; L4 bearer combat damage→each opponent
       loses 3. Frodo "must be blocked if able" enforced in pickBlockers.
     - PARTNER: Merry/Pippin tutor the named partner from library to hand.
     - POPULATE (+ Proliferate modal): Cayth auto-picks proliferate when
       counters exist, else copies the strongest token.
     - MANIFEST FLIP: manifest now stores manifestOriginal; Ugin's Mastery
       flips the best face-down creature face up (power ≥ 6 gate).
     - ANIMATE: originalType + boosts revert at turn handoff — Cacophony
       Unleashed (6/6 menace/deathtouch), Bespoke Battlewagon (printed P/T),
       Destiny Spinner (X/X land where X = enchantments).
     - GRAVEYARD-CAST: escape/unearth/jump-start simplified to an immediate
       cast of the best eligible card (Desdemona, Ghost Ark, Filigree Racer).
     - POISON: poisonCounters state + lose-at-10 SBA (Vraska).

KNOWN APPROXIMATIONS (documented, intentional)
──────────────────────────────────────────────
  - Server multiplayer has no rad-counter or hand-size context: Nightkin
    Ambusher stays blockable and Kefnet's gate is solo-enforced only.
    Forestwalk works everywhere (battlefield-only data).
  - Scourge's extra combat phase is not modeled (untap-all is).
  - Black Gate uses cantBeBlockedThisTurn (equivalent in solo 1v1).
  - Graveyard-cast mechanics fire immediately rather than granting a
    until-EOT window (toasted transparently).
  - Modal abilities auto-pick deterministically (toasted); no chooser UI.
  - Star Athlete: AI sacrifices only tokens/toughness≤2 creatures.
